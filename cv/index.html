Professional Summary

Theoretical physicist exploring how fundamental physical laws shape the limits of knowledge, communication, and computation. My current research focuses on device-independent and self-testing methods for certifying quantum resources, a crucial task for establishing trust and security in next-generation quantum technologies. Beyond quantum computing, I am also fascinated by AI and machine learning.
